# Automotive B2B2C / US public company

A selectable assessment profile for a US-based public automotive B2B2C SaaS company serving North America and Europe. This profile is a reusable perspective, not evidence about any particular company or its deployed systems. Activate through an explicit request, engagement context, or `profile: automotive-us-public` in a context file.

Use GCP-first managed-service preferences, forward-looking adoption, and functional/strategic mentoring. Include [public-company responsibilities](../references/public-company.md) and [automotive/market boundaries](../references/automotive-and-markets.md) in baseline reviews.

Resolve material unknowns: countries/states/provinces (including whether Mexico, UK or Switzerland are served); issuer/filer status; workflows; OEM/dealer/fleet/lender relationships; vehicle integration; data collected/inferred; financial-reporting dependencies; services/regions; contracts; team capacity/budget; recovery objectives; AI and data permissions.

North America is not synonymous with the US; Europe is not synonymous with the EU. Consumers, drivers, owners and lessees differ. Dealers may span organizational boundaries; privacy roles vary by purpose. Do not infer vehicle commands, financing, telemetry or safety obligations from the industry label.

Explicit engagement facts override this profile. Selecting it does not confirm applicability of a law, company control scope, a specific customer promise or operational effectiveness.
