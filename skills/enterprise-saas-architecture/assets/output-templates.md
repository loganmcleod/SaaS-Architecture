# Output templates

Use the template suited to the request; omit irrelevant detail and label omissions. These describe output fields, not fabricated example findings. Keep sensitive evidence in approved locations and link it with access-aware references.

## Baseline or deep dive

1. Decision-oriented opening: assessed scope, business consequence, strengths, top exposures/opportunities, confidence and decisions needed.
2. Context and architecture: actors, critical journeys, data/trust boundaries, dependencies, regions, role owners, environment and evidence period.
3. Coverage: discipline ID; disposition; evidence examined; limitations; follow-up. A baseline accounts for every D01–D20 plus X01 applicability; a deep dive lists only scoped disciplines and boundaries.
4. Findings: use the record below; group shared causes and separate evidence gaps from confirmed failures.
5. Tradeoffs: options, including current state, value/cost/risk, dependencies, reversibility and reasons for recommendation.
6. Roadmap: priority, action, role owner, dependencies, proposed timing/effort, success evidence and verification owner.
7. Leadership: targeted questions, delegated exercise, ownership decision and learning measure.
8. Sources and limitations: locations, dates/versions, assumptions and what could change the conclusion. State whether production effectiveness was verified.

### Finding record

- ID / title / kind / discipline tags / affected journey.
- Requirement or outcome; source and applicability basis.
- Observation and evidence references; separately, inference and business consequence.
- Implementation status; confidence and why; risk severity/likelihood when relevant, or opportunity hypothesis.
- Existing strengths/mitigations; residual exposure; unresolved evidence.
- Alternatives and tradeoffs; recommended proportionate action; accountable role; dependencies/effort.
- Acceptance evidence and verification owner; review trigger; decision required.

## Architecture decision record

Decision question; business context; confirmed requirements/constraints and assumptions; status quo; viable alternatives; comparison of customer value, fit, controls, geography, economics, team effort and reversibility; evidence and uncertainty; recommendation; migration/rollback; owner; review trigger and what would change the decision. Record proposed versus accepted status.

## Public-company control/applicability map

| Business/reporting process | System/data dependency | Obligation/control basis | Scope status and owner | Control design | Evidence / period / population | Exceptions / next decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use confirmed/candidate/out_of_scope/unknown scope with reasons. Describe design versus operating evidence separately. Route legal, accounting, audit and materiality conclusions to authorized roles. Do not label this a compliance certification.

## Technology radar and experiment charter

- Candidate, use case, owner, radar stage, evidence date and review trigger.
- Problem, baseline, measurable value hypothesis and alternatives.
- Current product/version/region/status and source; data use and permissions.
- Time/budget limits, permitted environment/data, representative workload.
- Success metrics, guardrails, stop/failure conditions, fallback and exit costs.
- Evaluation result, uncertainty, operating/control readiness and next adoption decision.
- Team learning/delegation objective and production ownership if adopted.

## Leadership coaching brief

Observed system condition and evidence; business impact; hypotheses about organizational causes (not assertions about people); diagnostic questions; decision principle to teach; delegated exercise; authority/support needed; observable success; follow-up trigger. Connect the exercise to the platform outcome, not merely training attendance.

## Executive investment note

Decision requested; customer/business opportunity or exposure; evidence confidence; status quo and alternatives; proposed investment/capacity; expected benefits and assumptions; dependencies and transition risks; accountable roles; milestone evidence; stop/reconsider criteria. Distinguish financial estimates from validated financial data and protect sensitive details.
